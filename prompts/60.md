0-5s: [Symmetrical wide shot, stable slow dolly forward] Camera smoothly enters from the ballroom entrance. In the left foreground, two women speak quietly, one holding a champagne glass. In the right foreground, two male guests smile and toast. In the midground, three dancing couples rotate elegantly across the black mirrored floor. On the distant upper balcony, a string quartet performs. A waiter passes the emerald-green banquet table carrying a silver tray of champagne glasses and fruit. Orange chandelier light, candles, flowers, and colorful gowns create rich reflections on the floor. Audio: gentle conversation, clinking glass, string music, and natural laughter.

5-8s: [Smooth lateral move with a slight push-in, no whip pan] Camera moves naturally from left to right, passing behind the shoulder of a woman in a royal-blue silk shawl, then enters the center of the party. Six featured guests form a relaxed, believable group: one dancing couple has just completed a turn, while two women and two men smile at one another and slowly raise their champagne glasses. Focus on natural eyeline exchanges, believable finger placement around the glasses, subtle breathing, and gentle movement in the fabric.
8-10s: [Brief slow motion, stable medium shot] The champagne glasses are about to touch. Camera continues an extremely slow push-in, first revealing candlelight reflected on the rims of the glasses, then a woman gently raising her chin and a male guest smiling toward a friend. The music and laughter enter a very subtle slow-motion feeling without stopping.

10-20s: [Collective time freeze, main visual sequence] At the exact moment the six champagne glasses touch for the first time, time in the entire ballroom is gently paused. This is not a frightening freeze frame. It is an extremely luxurious, joyful, and believable moment of celebration.

Every person stops naturally within their existing action: the central guests keep warm, authentic smiles and look naturally toward one another; a dancing couple in the center of the floor pauses halfway through a turn, with the gown held in an elegant, physically believable curve; the waiter freezes while pouring champagne, with one thin unbroken stream of champagne flowing from the bottle into the glass, realistic bubbles visible inside the liquid; several pink flower petals, a few tiny champagne droplets, and one grape slipping from the edge of the silver tray remain suspended in midair; chandelier reflections, candle flames, and glasses reflected on the banquet tables are completely still.

Only the camera is allowed to move. Camera follows an extremely stable, extremely slow semicircular path around the central toast group. Do not make a full 360-degree orbit, do not pass through bodies, and do not use fast zooms. The camera first glides past the suspended champagne stream and droplets, then passes the frozen silk skirt of a dancer, and finally returns to the faces and hands of the six central guests holding their glasses. During the freeze, sound becomes almost silent: the final note of the strings stretches and stops, leaving only a very soft, warm low room tone and delicate champagne-bubble texture. All frozen faces, hands, and clothing must remain stable without flickering.
