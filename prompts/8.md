## 📝 Prompt

```
SEEDANCE 2.5 CAN NOW DO SLAPSTICK AND THAT'S HARDER THAN IT LOOKS

A contestant balances across a spinning foam roller over a pool, wobbles, and wipes out into the water - a full Japanese game-show obstacle bit, crowd cheering in the stands. Comedy timing and a physical pratfall are genuinely tough for AI and this holds.

Most people test a new model on hero shots - dramatic, slow controlled. A game-show wipeout is the opposite: fast physical comedy, a body losing balance, a crowd reacting water splashing on impact. That's a much harder thing to make land.

Here's why a clip like this is a real test

1. Physical comedy depends on timing the failure. A pratfall only works if the wobble, the overbalance, and the fall happen in the right rhythm. Too smooth and it's not funny; too random and it's not believable
2. A reacting crowd has to sell it. Rows of spectators clapping and watching in the stands do the same job the crowd does in any live-event clip -the audience reaction is what makes it feel real
3. Water and impact are a physics stress test. Body hitting a surface, then a splash, is exactly the kind of contact-plus-fluid moment that used to fall apart instantly
4. It's a specific, recognizable format. Japanese game-show obstacle courses have a distinct look and energy -nailing the genre, not just a generic person falling, is the flex

Why this matters

• Comedy and physical failure are underrated benchmarks - everyone tests drama, far fewer test a convincing wipeout 
• The version leap is the real story: putting the Seedance 2.0 generation side by side is how you actually judge progress, not marketing claims 
• Physical comedy landing at all is a sign the model is handling timing and balance, not just pretty stills
Worth being straight about
• A side-by-side against the 2.0 version is the honest way to show a jump - a single clip alone proves less than the comparison does 
• A teaser clip is the best-case cut - the real test is running your own awkward, physical, timing-dependent scene through it

Everyone benchmarks new models on the dramatic hero shot. The quieter test is whether it can make you laugh at someone falling off a foam roller -and that's a harder problem than it looks.
```
